CCTV Monitor P0.14.3 — Queued Email Notifications & Delivery Reliability

FRESH INSTALL
1. Extract to a new folder.
2. Open PowerShell in that folder.
3. Run: .\FIRST-RUN-P0.14.3.cmd
4. Open http://localhost:3000 if the browser does not open automatically.

NORMAL START
.\START-CCTV-MONITOR.cmd

VERIFY SOFTWARE
.\VERIFY-P0.14.3-COMPLETE.cmd

QUALIFY EMAIL ALERTS
.\QUALIFY-P0.14.3.cmd

P0.14.3 adds queued SMTP email delivery on top of P0.14.2 rules. Email is opt-in per rule, SMTP is disabled until configured, SMTP passwords are encrypted locally, and failed deliveries retry without blocking camera/runtime work.
