# P0.13 PW8 Password Policy Hotfix

- Changed new/changed password policy from a 12-character minimum to **5–8 characters**.
- Applied consistently to first-time admin setup, account creation, admin reset, and My Account password change.
- Existing longer passwords remain valid for login until changed.
- No media, recording, MediaMTX, FFmpeg, camera, event, session, or database-schema behavior was changed.
