CCTV MONITOR P0.14 — COMPLETE FRESH-INSTALL PACKAGE

Fresh install:
  1. Extract to a new folder.
  2. Open PowerShell in the folder.
  3. Run:
       .\FIRST-RUN-P0.14.cmd

Normal startup after installation:
       .\START-CCTV-MONITOR.cmd

Software verification:
       .\VERIFY-P0.14-COMPLETE.cmd

Windows USB motion qualification:
       .\QUALIFY-P0.14.cmd

P0.14 keeps Motion Detection OFF by default. Enable it from Admin > Cameras after the USB live stream is stable.

Recommended first test:
  - Arm Continuous Recording.
  - Enable Motion Detection on the same USB camera.
  - Move clearly in view.
  - Open Events and find MOTION_DETECTED.
  - After the recording segment finalizes, use Find footage and Play.
